Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 25 October 2026

Flirt2Me has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion and any sexual content involving a minor.

Our standards

  • Flirt2Me is only for adults aged 18+. Users must give their date of birth at signup, and accounts under 18 are refused.
  • Any account that appears to belong to a minor, or that shares, requests or promotes CSAE/CSAM, is banned permanently.
  • Nudity is not allowed anywhere on Flirt2Me. Video calls and chat photos are checked automatically for nudity, and violations end the call, remove the content and lead to bans.
  • Users must keep their face visible on video, which helps our team spot minors and fake accounts.
  • Every chat, profile and video call has a Report button. Reports involving minors are reviewed with the highest priority.
  • We preserve evidence and report CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) and NCMEC where applicable, and we cooperate with law enforcement.

How to report

Use the Report button in the app, or email flirt2me.support@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety". If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police (112 in India) or Childline (1098).
