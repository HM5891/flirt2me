Questions

Is Yaari9 free?

Yes. The app is free, every random call starts with 2 free minutes, and you can earn free coins every day. You only pay if you want extras.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

We share the app directly from this website. Android shows a warning for any app installed outside the Play Store. Tap Allow from this source once, and updates come inside the app after that.

Who will I meet?

Real adults (18+) who are online at the same time as you. You can match with anyone, or choose women-only or men-only.

Is it safe?

Faces must be visible, nudity is detected automatically, and anyone can be reported or blocked with one tap. Calls are never recorded.

How do I pay for coins?

With any UPI app, like Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm. Coins are added after the payment is confirmed.

Does it work on iPhone?

Not yet. Yaari9 is for Android phones running Android 7.0 or newer.
