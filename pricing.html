How call time works

  • Every call shows a timer. Near the end, either person can tap Continue to add more minutes for both people.
  • If a call ends early, unused whole minutes are given back as coins automatically.
  • Free minutes are limited each day. On busy days they can run out and come back the next day; paid calls always work.
  • Happy Hour: on many days there is a Happy Hour (usually in the evening, India time) with more free minutes and cheaper gifts. The app shows when it is on.

Offers

During festivals and special days we may run offers such as extra coins on every pack, cheaper gifts, free profile boosts or bigger daily rewards. The banner in the app shows exactly what each offer gives and when it ends. Each offer runs only for the dates shown in the app.

Free coins

You can also earn coins for free through the daily check-in, daily missions, inviting friends, and partner streaks (keep chatting or calling a partner every day: 10 coins each at 7 days, 50 coins each at 30 days).

Prices in other countries

Outside India, the app shows coin pack prices for your country: in US dollars, and in many countries also in your local currency. Each country has its own prices, so they are not a simple conversion of the rupee price. Every price is all-inclusive: it already contains that country's taxes (such as VAT or GST) and all payment costs, and nothing is added at checkout. The exact price is always shown in the app before you pay.

You can pay with Skrill (card or Skrill balance, in USD or your local currency) or with Binance Pay (USDT). In India you can also pay with UPI.

Good to know
  • Coins are virtual credits for use inside the Yaari9 app only. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for money or transferred to another account.
  • Coins are delivered to your account after your payment is confirmed.
