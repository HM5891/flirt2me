How call time works

  • Every call shows a timer. Near the end, either person can tap Continue to add more minutes for both people.
  • If a call ends early, unused whole minutes are given back as coins automatically.
  • Free minutes are limited each day. On busy days they can run out and come back the next day; paid calls always work.

Offers

During festivals and special days we may run offers such as extra coins on every pack, cheaper gifts, free profile boosts or bigger daily rewards. The banner in the app shows exactly what each offer gives and when it ends. Offers never change call prices. Each offer runs only for the dates shown in the app.

Free coins

You can also earn coins for free through the daily check-in, daily missions and by inviting friends.

Good to know
  • Coins are virtual credits for use inside the Flirt2Me app only. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for money or transferred to another account.
  • Coins are delivered to your account after your payment is confirmed.
  • See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for refunds.
