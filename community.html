Community Rules

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 29 October 2026

Yaari9 is for meeting new people in a fun and respectful way. Breaking these rules can get you banned permanently.

  1. 18+ only. No minors, ever.
  2. Keep your face visible on video. No covering the camera or pointing it away.
  3. No nudity or sexual content on video, in photos or in messages. Flirting is fine; explicit content is not.
  4. Be respectful. No harassment, threats, hate speech, bullying or pressure.
  5. Respect privacy. Don't record, screenshot or share anyone's video, photos or messages.
  6. Stay on Yaari9. Don't ask for or share phone numbers, UPI IDs, links or social handles.
  7. No money requests, scams or selling. Never send money to people you meet here.
  8. Be real. Use your own photos and correct gender and age. No fake accounts or bots.
  9. Voice rooms too. Every rule applies in rooms and room chat. No explicit talk, no shouting people down, no spam. Hosts: use mute and remove fairly, not to bully.
  10. Keep your profile clean. No contact details, links or sexual content in your name, bio, prompts or voice intro.
  11. Rate honestly. Use thumbs down for rude or rule-breaking calls, not for someone who simply skipped you.
  12. Report and block anyone who breaks these rules — it's quick and anonymous. You can report from chats, profiles, video calls and voice rooms.
