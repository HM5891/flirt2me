Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flirt2Me ("Flirt2Me", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Flirt2Me mobile app and related services (the "Service"). Flirt2Me is operated from India by Harkishansingh. By using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Flirt2Me.

In short: we collect only what we need to run a safe 1:1 video chat app. Video and voice calls are not recorded. We never sell your personal data. You can delete your account at any time from the app.

1. Who can use Flirt2Me

Flirt2Me is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we close the account and delete their data. If you believe a minor is using Flirt2Me, email flirt2me.support@gmail.com.

3. App permissions

  • Camera and microphone – for video/voice calls, selfies and voice notes, only while you use these features.
  • Photos – only the photos you choose to send or set as your profile photo.
  • Notifications – to tell you about new messages, partner calls and requests. You can turn these off in your phone settings.

4. How we share data

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • People you interact with – your display name, profile photo, gender, verified badge, and the messages, photos, voice notes and live video you send to them.
  • Service providers who process data for us under their own security and privacy terms:
    • Supabase – database, login and file storage
    • Agora – real-time video and voice calls
    • Google (Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging) – login and push notifications
    • Sightengine – automatic nudity detection on call pictures and chat photos
    • A payment gateway – when coin purchases are launched
    These providers may process data on servers outside India.
  • Legal reasons – if required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect the safety of a person, including reporting child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, messages and media: while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account: your profile is removed and hidden from everyone immediately, and your remaining personal data (messages, photos, voice notes, selfies) is deleted within 30 days.
  • Safety records (reports, moderation flags and evidence, bans) may be kept for up to 180 days, or longer if needed for a legal case or to stop banned users from returning.
  • Coin and payment records may be kept as long as required by tax and accounting law.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws you can:

  • See and correct your profile at any time in the app (Me → Edit profile).
  • Delete your account in the app (Me → Delete account) or by email – see Delete your account.
  • Ask for a summary of your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a complaint by emailing us.

We reply within 30 days. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the Service to you.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to databases is restricted by row-level security so users can only read data they are allowed to see. Chat photos, voice notes and selfies are stored in private storage and opened only with short-lived links. No system is 100% secure; if a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as required by law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
