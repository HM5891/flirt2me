Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yaari9 ("Yaari9", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Yaari9 mobile app and related services (the "Service"). Yaari9 (formerly called Flirt2Me) is operated from India by Harkishansingh. By using the Service you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Yaari9.

In short: we collect only what we need to run a safe 1:1 video chat app. Video and voice calls are not recorded. We never sell your personal data. You can delete your account at any time from the app.

1. Who can use Yaari9

Yaari9 is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we close the account and delete their data. If you believe a minor is using Yaari9, email yaari9.support@gmail.com.

3. App permissions

  • Camera and microphone – for video/voice calls, voice rooms, selfies, voice notes and your voice intro, only while you use these features.
  • Photos – only the photos you choose to send or set as your profile photo.
  • Notifications – to tell you about new messages, partner calls, requests, likes, people you asked to be alerted about, and offers such as Happy Hour. You can turn these off in your phone settings.

4. How we share data

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Other users – your display name, profile photo, gender, age, verified badge, prompts, voice intro, status and (if you turn it on) your city are shown on your profile. People you chat or call with get the messages, photos, voice notes and live video you send them. In voice rooms, other members see your name and photo, hear you when you are on the mic and read your room chat. When you like or visit someone, they may see it (except visits while you are Invisible).
  • Service providers who process data for us under their own security and privacy terms:
    • Supabase – database, login and file storage
    • Agora – real-time video calls, voice calls and voice rooms
    • Google (Google Sign-In and Firebase Cloud Messaging) – login and push notifications
    • Sightengine – automatic nudity detection on call pictures and chat photos
    • Your UPI app and bank – UPI payments go directly to our UPI account; a payment gateway may be added later
    • Skrill (Paysafe) – card and Skrill payments in USD or local currencies; Skrill handles your card details, we never see them
    • Binance – for users who pay in USDT with Binance Pay; the payment goes directly to our Binance account
    These providers may process data on servers outside India.
  • Legal reasons – if required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect the safety of a person, including reporting child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, messages and media: while your account is active.
  • Voice room chat: deleted automatically after 2 days. Room minute counts: after 7 days.
  • Profile visits and call ratings: used for the last 30 days only.
  • "Notify me when someone is online" requests: expire after a few hours.
  • Voice intro: deleted when you remove it or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account: your profile is removed and hidden from everyone immediately, and your remaining personal data (messages, photos, voice notes, voice intro, selfies) is deleted within 30 days.
  • Safety records (reports, moderation flags and evidence, bans) may be kept for up to 180 days, or longer if needed for a legal case or to stop banned users from returning.
  • Coin and payment records may be kept as long as required by tax and accounting law.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws you can:

  • See and correct your profile at any time in the app (Me → Edit profile).
  • Delete your account in the app (Me → Delete account) or by email – see Delete your account.
  • Ask for a summary of your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a complaint by emailing us.

We reply within 30 days. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer provide the Service to you.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to databases is restricted by row-level security so users can only read data they are allowed to see. Chat photos, voice notes and selfies are stored in private storage and opened only with short-lived links. Voice intros are meant to be heard on your profile, so they are stored where anyone with the link can play them; do not say anything private in your voice intro. No system is 100% secure; if a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as required by law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
