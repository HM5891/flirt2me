Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy applies to all coin purchases made in the Yaari9 app. Please read it before buying coins.

1. Digital product

Coins are a digital product. They are added to your Yaari9 account after your payment is confirmed and can be used right away. Because of this, coin purchases are generally non-refundable and cannot be cancelled once the coins are delivered, except in the cases below.

2. When you will get a refund

  • Unused call time: if you paid to add minutes and the call ends early, the unused whole minutes are returned to your account as coins automatically — you do not need to ask.
  • Payment taken but coins not received: if money was debited but coins were not added to your account within 24 hours, we will either add the coins or refund the full amount.
  • Double payment: if you were charged twice for the same order, the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Wrong amount charged because of an error on our side: the difference is refunded.
  • Failed payment: if a payment failed but money was debited, it is usually returned automatically by your bank or UPI app within 5–7 working days. If it is not, contact us and we will help.

3. When refunds are not given

  • Coins that have already been used (matches, call time that was used, gifts, boosts, "Who liked you" unlocks, voice room minutes).
  • Unused coins after you change your mind about the purchase.
  • Coins in accounts that were banned for breaking our Terms of Service or Community Rules.
  • Coins remaining when you delete your account.
  • Free coins from daily rewards, missions, referrals or partner streaks.
  • Binance Pay payments sent to the wrong address or network, which we cannot receive or recover.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email yaari9.support@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with:

  • the email address of your Yaari9 account,
  • the date and amount of the payment,
  • the payment reference (UTR / transaction ID) and, if possible, a screenshot.

We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days (UPI refunds to your UPI account; Binance Pay refunds in USDT to your Binance account).

5. Cancellation

Yaari9 has no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel. You are only charged when you choose to buy a coin pack. You can stop using the app or delete your account at any time.
