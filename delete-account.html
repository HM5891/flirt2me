Delete your Flirt2Me account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Flirt2Me and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: by email

If you can't open the app, email flirt2me.support@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Immediately: your name, profile photo, bio, interests and languages are removed, your partners and requests are removed, and nobody can find or see your profile.
  • Within 30 days: your chat messages, photos, voice notes and verification selfie.
  • Kept for a limited time: safety records (reports, moderation flags, bans) for up to 180 days to protect other users and stop banned users from returning, and coin/payment records as long as tax law requires.
  • Unused coins are lost when an account is deleted.
